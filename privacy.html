2Summary

3Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub’s servers receive the technical information your browser sends with every request, such as your IP address, the date and time, the page or file requested, and your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the IP address of every visitor to a GitHub Pages site is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account.

I use GitHub Pages to deliver this website reliably and securely. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is a website that works and is protected against attacks. GitHub uses its logs for its own security purposes, as described in its privacy statement.

GitHub may process this data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR), and GitHub also uses the EU standard contractual clauses. GitHub does not publish a fixed retention period for these logs. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab).

4Contacting me

When you email me, call me, or send me a message prepared with the contact form, I process the information you give me: usually your name, email address, organisation and the content of your message. I use it only to reply to you.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in answering messages sent to me.

The contact form does not send anything to this website’s server. It prepares an email that you send from your own email account, so the message travels through your email provider and mine, like any other email.

I delete messages once they have been dealt with, unless the law requires me to keep them longer.

You don’t have to provide personal data to use this website. If you want a reply, I need at least a way to reach you.

5Theme setting in your browser

When you switch between light and dark mode, your choice (“light” or “dark”) is saved in your browser’s local storage under the name ik-theme, so the next page opens in the same theme. The value stays on your device, is never sent to me and cannot identify you.

Storing it is strictly necessary to provide the function you asked for, so no consent is required (§ 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

6Links to other websites

This website links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Hugging Face and Unsplash. These are ordinary links: nothing is loaded from those services and no data is sent to them unless you click a link. Once you are on their website, their own privacy policies apply.

7Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • find out which personal data I hold about you (Art. 15)
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • have processing restricted (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)

To use any of these rights, email me at ik_khan1@icloud.com.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for me is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW): www.ldi.nrw.de (opens in a new tab).

8Encryption

This website is delivered over an encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connection, shown by “https://” and the lock symbol in your browser’s address bar.

9Changes to this policy

I update this policy when the website or the law changes. The current version is always on this page.
